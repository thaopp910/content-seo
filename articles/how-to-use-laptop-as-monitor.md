# How to Use Laptop as Monitor: Easy Ways to Turn Your Laptop Into a Second Screen

A spare laptop already has a good screen, so buying a new monitor is not always necessary. This guide shows **how to use laptop as monitor** with four tested methods. In addition, it explains what each one is good for, how gamers use it, and how to fix the problems most people hit.

## What to Prepare Before Connecting a Laptop Display?

Most failed setups come from one wrong assumption. Many people expect the laptop's HDMI port to accept video, but it almost always sends video out only. So, using a laptop as a monitor needs a wireless link, an app, or a capture card.

Before you start, check these points:

- **Miracast support:** press **Windows + R**, run **dxdiag**, and click **Save All Information**. The saved file should say "Miracast: Available".
- **Network:** put both devices on the same 5 GHz Wi-Fi network. It handles video far better than 2.4 GHz.
- **Updates:** install the latest Windows or macOS updates and graphics drivers.
- **Power:** plug the laptop in, because receiving video drains the battery quickly.
- **Screen quality:** a Full HD panel makes a far better second screen than an old 1366 x 768 one.

Once these basics are ready, setting up a laptop as monitor takes only a few minutes.

## How to Use Laptop as Monitor?

Each method trades convenience for speed. Use this table to choose, then follow the steps below.

| Method | Best for | Cost | Delay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Miracast | Office work on Windows | Free | Medium |
| Capture card | Gaming and video | $20 to $150 | Very low |
| Third-party apps | Mixed systems | Free or paid | Medium |
| AirPlay or Sidecar | Apple users | Free | Low |

### Method 1: Connect Wirelessly Using Windows Miracast

[Miracast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miracast) is the quickest answer to how to use laptop as second monitor on Windows. It compresses the image, so text and slides look sharp while fast video may stutter.

1. On the laptop, open **Settings > System > Projecting to this PC**.
2. If the page is greyed out, add **Wireless Display** under **Settings > System > Optional features**.
3. Choose **Available everywhere** and turn on **Require PIN** for safety.
4. On the main PC, press **Windows + K** and select the laptop.
5. Press **Windows + P** and pick **Extend** to get a true second screen.

### Method 2: Use a Video Capture Card for Direct HDMI Input

A USB capture card gives the laptop the HDMI input it lacks. Cheap USB 2.0 cards usually top out at 1080p 30 fps. Meanwhile, USB 3.0 models handle 1080p 60 fps, which matters for games.

1. Connect the source device to the card with HDMI.
2. Plug the card into the laptop's USB port.
3. In OBS Studio, add a **Video Capture Device** source.
4. Right-click the preview and choose **Fullscreen Projector**.

### Method 3: Stream Displays via Third-Party Applications

Apps help when your devices run different systems. For example, spacedesk turns a Windows, Android, or iOS device into an extra screen over Wi-Fi or USB. Duet Display is a paid option for Mac and Windows. However, apps add a small delay, so keep them for documents, chat, or reference material.

### Method 4: Use AirPlay or Sidecar for Apple Devices

On Macs running macOS Monterey or later, the receiving MacBook can act as a display. First, turn on **AirPlay Receiver** in its sharing settings. Then, on the main Mac, open **Control Center > Screen Mirroring** and choose **Use As Separate Display**. Similarly, Sidecar extends a Mac onto an iPad.

## How Do Players Display Console Games on a Laptop Screen?

Gamers often use a laptop as a monitor when the family TV is busy or they are traveling. There are two routes, and each suits a different player.

- **Capture card:** this gives the lowest delay and works with PS5, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch. On a PS5, first turn off **Enable HDCP** in **Settings > System > HDMI**. Otherwise, the laptop shows a black screen.
- **Remote play:** PS Remote Play and the Xbox app on Windows stream games over your network. Connect the console with an Ethernet cable for steadier frames. Sony recommends at least 15 Mbps for a smooth stream.

In short, competitive players should choose a capture card. Casual players can start with remote play, since it costs nothing.

## What Common Mistakes Block External Screen Connections?

Small oversights cause most failed attempts to use a laptop as a monitor. Here is what to avoid and why.

- **Plugging a PC into the laptop's HDMI port:** the port only outputs, so nothing appears.
- **Using a guest network:** guest Wi-Fi often isolates devices, so they cannot find each other.
- **Skipping driver updates:** old graphics or Wi-Fi drivers often break Miracast.
- **Ignoring the firewall:** security software can block apps like spacedesk.
- **Leaving Duplicate mode on:** it mirrors one screen instead of adding space.
- **Expecting USB-C to accept video:** most laptop USB-C ports also send video out only.

Checking these points first saves a lot of guesswork later.

## How Can Users Fix Display Lag and Black Screen Issues?

Lag and black screens have different causes. Lag usually comes from the network, while a black screen points to settings or copy protection.

| Problem | Likely cause | Quick fix |
|---|---|---|
| Lag or stutter | Weak or busy Wi-Fi | Switch to 5 GHz or a USB cable |
| Black screen on capture card | HDCP is on | Disable HDCP on the source device |
| Black screen with Miracast | Wrong display mode | Press Windows + P and pick Extend |
| Blurry text | Resolution mismatch | Match the laptop's native resolution |
| Device not found | Separate networks or firewall | Join one network and allow the app |

If problems continue, restart both devices and reconnect them. Also, lowering the resolution to 1080p reduces lag on a laptop as second monitor setup.

## FAQs

### Does Any Laptop Work as a Monitor?

Almost any laptop can, but the method depends on its hardware. Windows laptops with Miracast work wirelessly. Older models need an app or a capture card.

### Is It Possible to Connect a Laptop to a PC With HDMI?

Not directly in most cases, because laptop HDMI ports only send video out. Instead, use a USB capture card to receive the PC's signal.

### How Does a Laptop Work as a Monitor Without Wi-Fi?

Use a wired route. A capture card or spacedesk over USB works offline. This is how to use a laptop as a monitor where networks are weak.

### Which Method Works Best for PS5 or Xbox?

A USB 3.0 capture card offers the lowest delay. Alternatively, remote play apps work well on a strong home network.

## Conclusion

Now you know **how to use laptop as monitor** for work and play. Miracast and AirPlay suit office tasks, while a capture card is best for gaming. Start with the free option that fits your devices, and upgrade only if lag gets in the way.
